About

Research with an engineering purpose.

I am a mechanical engineering researcher and educator with a background in aeronautical engineering. My work focuses on magnesium-based composites and solid-state hydrogen storage, linking materials processing and characterization with performance and practical applications.

From March 2024 to August 2026, I served as Postdoctoral Researcher and Project Manager at the ATW Solid-State Hydrogen Energy Research Center, National Taiwan University of Science and Technology (NTUST). My responsibilities combined hydrogen storage research, laboratory development, graduate mentorship, and coordination with industry.

My experience also includes undergraduate teaching, curriculum development, academic administration, and international research collaboration.

Research focus

Materials for energy and performance.

01 / Hydrogen storage

Magnesium-based solid-state storage

Hydrogen sorption capacity, kinetics, and catalytic enhancement of magnesium alloys using high-entropy alloys, carbon-based materials, and other catalytic systems.

02 / Advanced composites

Microstructure and mechanical behavior

Magnesium matrix nanocomposites, nanoscale reinforcements, heat treatment, and severe plastic deformation through equal-channel angular pressing.

03 / Research translation

From laboratory to prototype

University-industry research coordination, experimental infrastructure, equipment procurement, and prototype development for hydrogen energy applications.
