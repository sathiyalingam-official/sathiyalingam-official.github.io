01 / About

Research, Education, and Industry Collaboration.

I am a mechanical engineering academic with more than 14 years of combined teaching and research experience across India and Taiwan. My work spans magnesium-based composites, solid-state hydrogen storage, high-entropy alloy catalysts, severe plastic deformation, and sustainable energy materials.

From March 2024 to August 2026, I served as Postdoctoral Researcher and Project Manager at the ATW Solid-State Hydrogen Energy Research Center at NTUST. I coordinated university-industry research activities, supported laboratory infrastructure and prototype development, and mentored graduate researchers.

My CV lists 31 peer-reviewed journal articles, 20 corresponding-author publications, and two international book chapters. I have mentored 18 graduate researchers and contributed to research programs totaling approximately NT$61 million.

My academic responsibilities have included curriculum development, placement coordination, research administration, equipment procurement, and international collaboration.

03 / Experience

Research, Teaching, and Leadership.

March 2024 – August 2026

Postdoctoral Researcher & Project Manager

National Taiwan University of Science and Technology (NTUST), Taiwan

Served as a Postdoctoral Researcher and Project Manager at the ATW Solid-State Hydrogen Energy Research Center, integrating advanced research in magnesium-based solid-state hydrogen storage with university-industry project management, graduate research mentorship, laboratory development, and technology-oriented collaboration with ATW Technology.

Coordinated the execution of a major university-industry collaborative research program as Project Manager funded by ATW Technology, valued at approximately NT$60 million. Played a key role in establishing and operationalizing the ATW Solid-State Hydrogen Energy Research Center at NTUST, coordinating research infrastructure, equipment procurement, manpower allocation, scheduling, and project implementation. Mentored Master's and Ph.D. researchers in experimental design, materials processing, hydrogen storage characterization, scientific writing, conference presentation, and research project execution.

April 2023 – February 2024

Associate Professor, Department of Aeronautical Engineering

Dhanalakshmi Srinivasan Engineering College, India

Contributed to undergraduate engineering education, departmental research development, and advanced materials research within the Department of Aeronautical Engineering.

Served as Research and Development Coordinator for the department, supporting faculty and student research activities. Conducted research on the microstructural and mechanical behavior of heat-treated AA8079-based hybrid composites.

February 2019 – February 2023

Doctoral Research Scholar, Nano and Micro Composite Laboratory

National Taiwan University of Science and Technology (NTUST), Taiwan

Conducted doctoral research on magnesium-based nanocomposites, with emphasis on materials processing, microstructural evolution, mechanical behavior, and hydrogen storage performance.

Fabricated magnesium-based nanocomposites using stir casting and investigated the influence of nanoscale reinforcement on microstructure and mechanical properties. Developed research experience in hydrogen storage characterization using the Sieverts apparatus.

August 2011 – February 2019

Assistant Professor, Department of Aeronautical Engineering

Sri Ramakrishna Engineering College, India

Served for more than seven years in undergraduate engineering education, academic administration, laboratory coordination, student development, curriculum activities, and applied engineering research.

Served as Placement Coordinator for the Department of Aeronautical Engineering, supporting student career development and industry engagement. Contributed to curriculum development under the Choice-Based Credit System (CBCS). Organized conferences, technical seminars, guest lectures, student activities, and industry-oriented academic programs.
